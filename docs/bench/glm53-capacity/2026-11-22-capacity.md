# GLM-5.3: what a position of context costs, and Cohorts (2026-11-22)

## Verdict

- **The "unexplained ~100 KB per position" is prompt-sweep workspace, not cache.**
  - At engine `6866989` (the launcher's pin), each position of capacity costs **196,422.5 B on each
    GPU**: the caches take 95,232 B, and the sweep buffers take 101,190.5 B.
  - The sweep buffers are sized by the capacity because `Decoder::prefill` ran a whole prompt as one
    sweep, and a prompt may be as long as the capacity.
  - The code predicts the measured Slot drop between capacities 4,096 and 65,536 to within 0.05 Slots
    per layer (16.05 predicted, 16 measured).
- **The fix is the Cohort,** on branch `glm53/chunked-sweeps`.
  - A longer prompt is read as one sweep per Cohort of `DecoderConfig::cohort` tokens (default 16,384,
    a multiple of the wave), and the sweep buffers are sized for `min(cohort, capacity)` tokens.
  - Context then costs only its caches, 95.3 KB a position.
  - At today's 64K this predicts **~89 Slots per Sparse layer instead of 83**. It reaches **~118K at
    today's 83** and **~268K at the 64-Slot floor**, all at BF16 KV.
- **Not yet run on a GPU.** The GPUs were serving another model. The gates are listed under "Gates
  still owed".

## Method

A static audit of every device allocation in the GLM-5.3 serve binary at `6866989`:

- `src/engine/glm53/decoder.cu` (`Decoder::open`);
- `src/cuda/glm53/attention_layer.cu` (`DeviceAttention::upload`, `LayerScratch::create`).

Every allocation's size was written out as a formula in the capacity C. The struct sizes were checked
with a host compile (`sizes.cpp`: `moe_plan::Plan` 2,376 B, `cuda::GemvJob` 64 B,
`moe::TokenSlots` 12 B). Nothing ran on a GPU.

**Checked and found not to grow with C:**
- the Selection (sized by the wave);
- RoPE (angles are computed in the kernel);
- the attention partials (sized by the Round);
- `LayerScratch` apart from `scores_`;
- logits and the head;
- Checkpoints (pageable host memory);
- MTP (no state at this commit).

**Against the receipts** (`docs/bench/glm53-serve/`):

| capacity | Slots per Sparse layer | receipt |
|---|---|---|
| 4,096 | 99 | `2026-11-21-serve-smoke.err` |
| 65,536 | 83 | `2026-11-21-maya-e2e-engine.err.txt` |

- **Predicted drop:** 16.05 Slots (`per_position.js`).
- **Precision of the receipts:** Slots are rounded down, so they bound the cost to 183.6–208.1 KB a
  position.
- **The "16K → 97" figure** in the vault's 128K estimate has no receipt. The code predicts 95–96.

**Compared with other engines:** vLLM and SGLang keep GLM-5.3 at 92.6 KB a position per GPU at BF16
(the Latent plus FP8 index keys on the 21 Full layers, replicated under tensor parallelism), plus
2.3–5.8 KB of prefill workspace. Our cache term is the same design with BF16 keys.

## The change: Cohorts

- **`core/glm53/cohort` (pure):**
  - `CohortSize::fit(at_most, wave)` takes the most whole waves that fit in the setting, so the default
    16,384 suits every legal wave, including ones that do not divide it, such as 24.
  - `cohorts(n, size)` splits a prompt into Cohorts that tile it in order, each full but the last,
    each starting at a multiple of the wave.
- **`Decoder::open`** fits `DecoderConfig::cohort` to the wave and sizes the sweep buffers for
  `min(cohort, capacity)` tokens.
- **`Decoder::prefill`** runs `State::sweep` once per Cohort, at positions `length + first`, then
  advances `length` once at the end. A failure still breaks the decoder, as before.
  - **Logits:** the head runs after each Cohort, since the next sweep reuses the rows. A Cohort's
    Rounds are its logits; the prompt's last Round gives the last token's.
  - **Capture:** each Cohort's capture lands at its tokens' rows.
- **Tools:** `--cohort K` on `ds4n-glm53-decode` and `ds4n-glm53-serve`.

**Why the bits are the same** (the argument the GPU gates must confirm):

1. **Waves and Rounds are unchanged.**
   - Every Cohort starts at a multiple of the wave, so its waves are the whole-prompt sweep's waves,
     with the same offsets.
   - The wave is a multiple of 8, so the Rounds are the same too.
   - The attention therefore runs the same launches over the same rows.
2. **Later Cohorts read the same cache rows.** A later Cohort attends the earlier Cohorts' cache rows
   exactly as a later wave attends the earlier waves': those rows are already written, at the same
   positions.
3. **Each token's MoE arithmetic is unchanged.**
   - Every GEMV column depends only on its own token.
   - A token's combine order is by group (ascending Expert, then the shared Expert), whatever other
     tokens share its groups.
   - A prompt routes exactly, so routing does not depend on residency.
4. **The Expert cache ends in the same state.** It records the same Rounds in the same order, so its
   final state, and the residency λ later sees, is the same. The RAM tier caps each layer on its own
   and routing never reads it.

**Unchanged:**
- Each Cohort after the first gathers each Sparse layer's Experts again, from the Slots, the RAM tier
  or the store.
- A prompt of up to one Cohort runs exactly as before.

**How far each plan reaches** (`reach.js`):

| plan | context at 83 Slots | context at the 64-Slot floor |
|---|---|---|
| Cohorts alone | 118K | 268K |
| Cohorts + the Latent rows split by position across the GPUs (next) | ~245K | ~545K |

**What a Cohort costs:** each Cohort after the first re-gathers each layer's Experts (~18 s of reads,
warm, per the waves report). At 16K that is roughly balanced against ~23 s of GPU work, so a long
prompt reads somewhat slower. This is inferred, and is measured in Gate 3.

## Considered and not done

**`smid` aliasing `sx`** (−24.6 KB a token) and **routing per wave** (−6.1 KB a token): before
Cohorts these were worth 2 GB at 64K. After them they save only a Cohort's share: 0.50 GB per GPU at
16K, ~0.66 Slots per layer.

- The alias also needs `__restrict__` removed from (or an in-place twin of) the residual and finish
  kernels. A GPU-unverified change to exact kernels is not worth 0.66 Slots.
- Left for when the Latent split is built.

## Gates still owed (need a GPU window)

1. **Identical bits across Cohort sizes.** Run `ds4n-glm53-decode --prefill sweep --logits-hash` on a
   prompt of ~2.5K tokens three times:
   - `--cohort 512` (5 Cohorts);
   - `--cohort 1024`;
   - the default (1 Cohort).
   All three hashes must be equal, and equal to `--prefill rounds`.
2. **Against the oracle.** Run `ds4n-glm53-decode --check --cohort 512` on a prompt of 1,100–1,600
   tokens (3 Cohorts), and once more under `--lambda 0.03 --generate 16`, so λ decode is replayed
   against the residency the Cohorts left. Every layer must be bit-identical.
3. **Memory and speed.**
   - Open at `--capacity 65536` with and without `--cohort 65536`, and log the Slots. Predicted: 89
     vs 83.
   - Time a 40K-token prompt at Cohorts of 8K, 16K and 32K against one Cohort, to choose the default.
4. **Serving.** The launcher's `DS4N_COMMIT` moves only after gates 1–3. Nothing in production changes
   before that.

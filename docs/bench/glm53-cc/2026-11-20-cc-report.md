# Cache-conditional routing (λ) on GLM-5.3: quality against speed

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Runs** (each about 2½–2¾ h), on the same corpus, baseline and cache
model:

| Run | λ values | Container | Seconds | Engine at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sweep (`2026-11-20-run.sh`) | 0.001–0.03 | `glm53-cc` | 9,910 | `71882c4` |
| control and λ 0.1 (`../glm53-slim/2026-11-20-run.sh`) | 0.1 | `glm53-slim` | 9,934 | `bfbbfd3` |
| knee (`2026-11-20-gen-run.sh`, 2nd half) | 0.05–0.2 | `glm53-cc2` | 9,169 | `bfbbfd3` |

Free generation at λ 0.03 / 0.1 / 0.2 on the chat prompts is its own report,
`2026-11-20-gen-report.md`.

## Question

Exact routing at the ~33% Expert residency two 96 GB cards allow misses about 29% of Expert
picks, which the cost model puts at ~5 tok/s (`../glm53-locality/2026-11-20-locality-report.md`).
Cache-conditional routing adds a bonus λ to the selection score of Experts already in VRAM, so
near-ties go to resident Experts. How many misses does each λ save, and what does it cost in
output quality?

## Method

- **Corpus:** the locality corpus, 4 teacher-forced chat sessions × 700 tokens (2,800
  positions), Backbone A (uncensored).
- **Streams:** `exact` (the Reference configuration's MoE, the baseline) and `exact-ccλ`. MoE
  on GPU 0, bit-exact to the CPU.
- **Control:** `exact-fp32-exchange`, a rounding-only departure that changes no choice of
  Experts directly. It measures what any small perturbation costs on these positions.
- **The router's view of the cache:** each stream keeps an Expert cache model per Sparse layer,
  LRU over 84 Slots (`core/glm53/layer_expert_cache`, ~33% of 256). Tokens are recorded in
  position order, one at a time, as decode would. A `-cc` stream's router adds λ for the Experts
  resident in its own model (`exact::RouteChoice`).
- **Quality:** KL(exact ‖ it) over the full vocabulary at every prompt position, top-1
  agreement, and perplexity of the corpus text.
- **Speed:** the oracle's own miss share (each session's cache starting empty, positions 32 on).
  Then a replay of each stream's Routes through `expert_locality.py`'s `lru-layer` policy: the
  same cache model, the sessions in one cache, an 18% RAM tier and the speed note's cost model
  (`tools/glm53-golden/route_choice_table.py`).
- **Why per-layer LRU:** it is the cache the oracle can run in its layer-major order, and on
  GLM it tracks a global LRU cache within 0.3 points (29.32% against 29.09% at 33%).

## Findings

1. **λ is a smooth dial from ~5 to ~45 tok/s (est.).** Misses fall from 29% to 15% at λ 0.03,
   6.7% at 0.1 and 3.1% at 0.2. λ ≤ 0.01 buys little (≤ 6.9 tok/s).
2. **The quality cost sits on a floor.** The control, a rounding-only change, costs KL 0.0449
   and 90.7% top-1. λ 0.001–0.002 cost exactly that. Any small perturbation that propagates
   through 75 layers costs about 0.045 on these positions, so λ ≤ 0.005 is indistinguishable
   from a rounding change. (λ 0.001 can change a choice only on the 22.6% of Routes whose
   margin is under 0.001, and only where a resident runner-up meets a non-resident 8th choice.)
3. **Where the knee is.** Above the floor, KL grows about linearly up to λ 0.15: +0.023 at 0.03,
   +0.079 at 0.1, +0.116 at 0.15. Then it steepens: +0.18 at 0.2, with the first-100-position
   KL jumping from 0.34 to 0.56. Perplexity stays within +2.2% through λ 0.1, not monotonically
   (+1.7% at 0.05, +2.2% at 0.07, +1.5% at 0.1), then reaches +3.5% at 0.15 and +4.6% at 0.2. On cold short chat prompts, λ 0.2 already reaches KL 0.82 and
   perplexity +40%, while λ 0.1 keeps exact's perplexity (`2026-11-20-gen-report.md`).
4. **The excess is front-loaded.** The control's own KL is higher early too (0.075 in the
   first 100 positions, ~0.04 after). λ's excess over it is largest there as well: the cache is
   filling, and everything resident was used moments ago.
5. **Why λ must be this small:** the median margin between the 8th and 9th choice is 0.0036.
   λ 0.03 can alter 96% of Routes, λ 0.1 99.9%. The smoke run's λ 10 (only resident Experts)
   destroyed the output (KL 5.9, perplexity 84,000).

## Recommendation

- **λ 0.1 as the fast setting:** est. **~23 tok/s** (4.4× exact), perplexity +1.5%, 86% top-1
  agreement. In free generation it writes correct answers, and on cold chat prompts it keeps
  exact's perplexity.
- **λ 0.05 as the conservative setting:** ~14 tok/s, KL 0.090.
- **λ 0.15 at most:** ~34 tok/s at +3.5% perplexity. Past it the cost steepens.
- **Stay exact (λ 0)** wherever the output must be the reference's.

## What this does not show

- **Task quality at scale:** KL and perplexity on 2,800 teacher-forced positions, plus 3 free
  generations, are proxies. No benchmark was run.
- **The cost model at high λ:** its constants (7.6 ms base, 0.35 ms per RAM hit, 1.6 ms per
  store read) are the speed note's estimates, not measured on a GLM engine, which does not
  exist yet. At λ ≥ 0.1 the 7.6 ms base is a third or more of the token time, so those rates
  depend most on it.
- **The engine's real cache:** the engine's cache is DS4.1's global decay cache, not a
  per-layer LRU. On exact routing the two are within 0.3 points.

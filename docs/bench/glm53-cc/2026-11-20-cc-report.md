# Cache-conditional routing (λ) on GLM-5.3: quality against speed

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Run:** `2026-11-20-run.sh` (container `glm53-cc`, 9,910 s).
**Engine:** `ds4n-glm53-oracle-run` at `71882c4` (branch `glm53/engine-core`).

## Question

Exact routing at the ~33% Expert residency two 96 GB cards allow misses about 29% of Expert
picks, which the cost model puts at ~5 tok/s (`../glm53-locality/2026-11-20-locality-report.md`).
Cache-conditional routing adds a bonus λ to the selection score of Experts already in VRAM, so
near-ties go to resident Experts. How many misses does each λ save, and what does it cost in
output quality?

## Method

- **Corpus:** the locality corpus, 4 teacher-forced chat sessions × 700 tokens (2,800
  positions), Backbone A (uncensored).
- **Streams:** `exact` (the Reference configuration's MoE, the baseline) and `exact-ccλ` for
  λ = 0.001, 0.002, 0.005, 0.01, 0.03. MoE on GPU 0, bit-exact to the CPU.
- **The router's view of the cache:** each stream keeps an Expert cache model per Sparse layer,
  LRU over 84 Slots (`core/glm53/layer_expert_cache`, ~33% of 256). Tokens are recorded in
  position order, one at a time, as decode would. A `-cc` stream's router adds λ for the Experts
  resident in its own model (`exact::RouteChoice`).
- **Quality:** KL(exact ‖ it) over the full vocabulary at every prompt position, top-1
  agreement, and perplexity of the corpus text.
- **Speed:** the oracle's own miss share (each session's cache starting empty, positions 32 on).
  Then a replay of each stream's Routes through `expert_locality.py`'s `lru-layer` policy: the
  same cache model, the sessions in one cache, an 18% RAM tier and the speed note's cost model
  (`tools/glm53-golden/route_choice_table.py`).
- **Why per-layer LRU:** it is the cache the oracle can run in its layer-major order, and on
  GLM it tracks a global LRU cache within 0.3 points (29.32% against 29.09% at 33%).

## Findings

1. **λ 0.03 halves the misses and about doubles the estimated rate**: 29% → 15%, 5.2 → 10.2
   tok/s. λ ≤ 0.01 buys little (≤ 6.9 tok/s).
2. **The quality cost sits on a floor (confirmed).** λ 0.001 can change a choice only on the
   22.6% of Routes whose margin is under 0.001, and only where a resident runner-up meets a
   non-resident 8th choice; it saves 1.1 points of misses. Yet its KL is already 0.045. The
   control, a rounding-only change of the Exchange, costs the same: 0.0449, 90.7% top-1. Any
   small perturbation costs about this much on these positions. Above it, λ 0.03 adds ~0.023
   mean KL (p90 0.106 → 0.161), and λ 0.1 adds ~0.079.
3. **Perplexity moves by under 1% and not monotonically** (10.73 → 10.82 at both λ 0.001 and
   0.03, 10.74 at 0.01): noise at this corpus size.
4. **The excess is front-loaded.** The control's own KL is higher early too (0.075 in the
   first 100 positions, about 0.04 after). λ's excess over it is highest there as well (λ 0.03:
   +0.04 early, +0.01–0.02 later): the cache is filling, and everything resident was used
   moments ago.
5. **Why λ must be this small:** the median margin between the 8th and 9th choice is 0.0036.
   λ 0.03 can alter 96% of Routes, λ 0.01 77%. The smoke run's λ 10 (only resident Experts)
   destroyed the output (KL 5.9, perplexity 84,000).

## What this does not show

- **The knee between λ 0.03 and 0.2,** and free generation: `2026-11-20-gen-run.sh` (chat
  prompts at λ 0.03 / 0.1 / 0.2, 64 tokens; then λ 0.05 / 0.07 / 0.15 / 0.2 on this corpus).
- **Task quality.** KL and perplexity on 2,800 teacher-forced positions are proxies. Free
  generation, long answers and benchmarks are not measured.
- **The engine's real cache.** The engine's cache is DS4.1's global decay cache, not a per-layer
  LRU. On exact routing the two are within 0.3 points.
- **The cost model's constants** (7.6 ms base, 0.35 ms per RAM hit, 1.6 ms per store read) are
  the speed note's estimates, not measured on a GLM engine, which does not exist yet.
